Two dials for Stream Deck +

Turn for volume or for previous / next track. Press or tap to play and pause.

Volume Dial
Rotate to change volume by your chosen step. The bar is green and turns red toward full volume.
Transport Dial
Rotate for previous or next track, press or tap to play and pause, and see the current track on the dial.
